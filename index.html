Waypoint · Direction

Research

My thesis work builds hierarchical deep-RL navigation for a real 6×6 skid-steer UGV: a latent world model for sample efficiency, per-wheel velocity control for slip compensation, and optimization-based safety filters so that learning failures never reach the actuators. The question underneath all of it: how should safety be enforced when policies are trained inside learned world models?

Safe RL & safety filters

Constraint-projection filters, constrained learning, and where safety belongs in the training loop for field robots.

World models for field robots

Latent dynamics models (DreamerV3-style) that stay honest about physics — slip, terrain, and disturbance.

Optimal control & NMPC

Nonlinear MPC for planning and tracking on skid-steer platforms, from CasADi/IPOPT formulations to field trials.

GPS-denied state estimation

Multi-sensor EKF fusion that holds a state estimate through GPS dropout, jamming, and degraded signal.
